Privacy Policy for Mero Hostel

Effective Date: January 25, 2026

Mero Hostel (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (“App”) collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we may collect basic details such as your name, email address, phone number, hostel name, room number, and role (e.g., admin or student), only as required for hostel management features.
  • Usage Data: We may collect non-personal information such as device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and general usage statistics to improve app stability and performance.
  • No Payment Data: We do not collect or store any payment card or banking information within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for legitimate app-related purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing hostel management features (rooms, students, expenses, attendance, and permissions).
  • Sending important service-related notifications.
  • Improving app functionality, performance, and security.
  • Fixing bugs and enhancing user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

User data is securely stored on protected servers or trusted third-party services (such as Firebase or similar backend platforms). We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We may share limited non-personal data (such as app usage analytics) with trusted analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase) to improve the app.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data stored in Mero Hostel.

  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.
  • To submit a deletion request, please navigate to:
    https://www.hostelorbit.com/delete-account
  • Alternatively, you may also email us at hostelorbit204@gmail.com .

  • Some minimal data may be retained temporarily in backups or logs for legal, security, or operational reasons, after which it will be automatically removed.
